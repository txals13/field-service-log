Privacy Policy

Field Service Log · last updated 3 October 2026

Field Service Log is a single-page application used by a field service technician to write up service visits and produce reports. It runs entirely in your browser. There is no server of ours. We operate no database, no analytics and no tracking of any kind, and we cannot see your data.

What the app stores, and where

  • On your device. Sessions, entries, photos and settings are kept in your browser's own storage (IndexedDB and localStorage). Clearing the site's data removes them.
  • In your Google Drive, if you sign in. Reports, photos, videos, voice notes and a session.json are written to a Field Service Log folder in the Drive of the account you sign in with. They are your files, in your account.

What the app is allowed to touch in your Drive

The app asks for the drive.file scope only. This is the narrowest Drive permission Google offers: it grants access solely to the files the app itself creates, plus any file you explicitly hand to it through the Google file picker. The app cannot see, list or read the rest of your Drive, and no part of it is designed to try.

What leaves your device

  • To Google Drive: the files listed above, when you are signed in.
  • To the Google Gemini API: the text of entries and notes, and only when you ask for it — by pressing the spell-check button, or by exporting a report in a language other than the one it was written in. Photos, videos and voice notes are never sent. If the report language matches the language you wrote in, nothing is sent at all. The API key used is your own, entered on your device and kept in your browser.
  • To Google's fonts and script CDNs: the libraries the app loads to draw PDFs and Word files, which is an ordinary web request and carries none of your content.

Nothing else leaves the device. There is nowhere else for it to go.

Google user data and limited use

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data is used only to provide the features described above, is never sold, never transferred to anyone except as needed to provide those features, and is never used for advertising or for training generalised models.

Keeping and deleting your data

You hold the data, so you control how long it is kept. Delete a session in the app and it goes from the device; delete the Field Service Log folder in Drive and the files go with it. Revoking the app's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions ends its Drive access immediately.

Children

This is a tool for professional use and is not directed at children.
